A quantity obtained by ANALYZING something — a model, its solve, a derived loss, simulated results, or empirical data — rather than by transforming a recorded trajectory. NodeSelection

datamodel.schema.NodeSelection(
    keep=empty_list(),
    drop=empty_list(),
    applied=None,
)

The nodes of a network to keep or to drop, by label; exactly one of keep and drop is stated. A label is matched as written or through the aliases a by_label reconciliation uses (a node’s alternateName and the atlas terminology). A selection is a set: the kept nodes stay in the order the network carries them, whatever order keep lists them in, and are renumbered from 0. The selection is applied when the network is resolved, after its source has delivered nodes and arrays and before anything else reads them. Every stored layer, edge-parameter matrix and per-node array (nodes/<name>) is restricted to the kept nodes, an edge listed between two nodes goes with either of its nodes, and number_of_nodes is the kept count. Transforms, delays and couplings therefore see the selected network only: a normalisation by the largest weight is taken over the kept pairs. A layer or argument read through used: is read for the selected nodes: with reconcile: by_label it is aligned to them by name, without it the array has to come in the selected node count. A layer a producer: builds is not cut and has to come in the selected node count, and a per-node parameter read by source and measure from a network with more nodes is aligned to the kept nodes by label. A warning names the network and both counts whenever a selection drops nodes from what the network carries; read back beside arrays that are already the selected ones, it drops none and gives none. Refused, naming what is wrong: a label the network does not carry, keep beside drop, a label listed twice, a selection that leaves no node, a node declared by label that the selection drops, and a network that carries a mesh or a route to another network, whose arrays the selection cannot restrict.

PropagationGate

datamodel.schema.PropagationGate(direction=None, mask=None, min_dispersion=None)

A propagation requirement on a grouped wave reduction. A per-timestep wave test cannot tell a standing field from a travelling one, since a standing field repeats the same spatial pattern every timestep. The reduction sums the per-element unit direction vector over the post-transient timesteps and computes each group’s direction dispersion, the mask-weighted mean over elements of 1 - |time-mean direction|: 0 for a direction field fixed in time, growing as the direction moves. A group whose dispersion falls below min_dispersion has no travelling waves, so its proportion of waves is 0 and its proportion-directed and rho are undefined (NaN).

    schema_version=None,
)

A project’s whole look in one declarable object: its colours (inherited from Palette), the geometry a style sheet used to own — tick shape, axis weight, line weights, legend, grid, type — and the rule its axes follow: who places the ticks, where an axis ends, which spines are drawn and whether they meet. Declared on a study (SimulationStudy.theme) it is the one rule every figure of that study is drawn by; declared on a figure it states only that figure’s differences. Either way it beats every layer in Figure.style, so a look a spec states cannot be quietly overridden by a sheet underneath it. It is the reason a study need not ship a .mplstyle: a sheet is a backend’s vocabulary written outside the spec, where nothing validates it and no other backend can read it. TVB-O’s own is curated at tvbo:themes/default; a project names that in iri and states only what it changes, exactly as an Observation or a Coupling reuses a curated one. A slot left unset is not a value — the layer underneath keeps it, so a theme states the look it means to fix and nothing else.
